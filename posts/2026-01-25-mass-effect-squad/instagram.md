The squad that carried the weight of the galaxy (and a lot of bad decisions) 🌌

Here's my take on bringing together our favorite characters, each with their own read and rhythm, but unified through a shared basing scheme and color balance. I leaned into contrast across the team, cool blues against warm reds, cleaner armor panels offset by battle wear, and just enough glow to hint at biotics and tech without overwhelming the figures. Group shots like this are a good forcing function, they quickly show where consistency works and where individual choices still need tightening.

Painted with a mix of patience, iteration, and a healthy respect for the source material. On to the next mission.
