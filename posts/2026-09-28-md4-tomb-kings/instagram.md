Four Tomb Guardians. Regeneration. Armour like a castle wall. ☀️

Matchday 4 for the Holy Hand Grenadiers was a blazing-sunny day against the Tomb Kings Entourage, and that wall had a name: Khakters the Exalted. He folded squire Elma Kuchler before the ball even landed on turn 1, then came back for Miltrud and Kunigunde later.

Meanwhile their thrower Takatenus strolled in for a touchdown on turn 8, and again on the final turn. 0–2.

We didn't take down a single Guardian all game. Regeneration will do that. Silver lining: knight catcher Alison ran all over that pitch and stole the MVP off a losing side. And Miltrud walked away with a brand new Hatred (Undead), which honestly feels earned.

Also, just look at the gold on these Tomb Kings. Stunning team to lose to.
