Maestro

Maestro’s not afraid to rock some color—those purples and greens pop harder than a gamma bomb—but don’t let the flashy wardrobe fool you. This isn’t a fashion statement, it’s a warning. Take a closer look. This Variant-Hulk from Earth-9200 is already standing atop what’s left of the Earth’s mightiest heroes. 💀🟣💚

This marks mini #150 for me! It’s been a fun and rewarding journey—I’ve learned a ton, improved my technique, and picked up more tools (and brushes) than I can count. But if there’s one thing I’ve figured out, it’s how much more there still is to learn. Thanks for following along!

You can see my full painting log and more photos over at lefthandedminis.com.
