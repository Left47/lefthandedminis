Emma Frost

Diamond-hard resolve and impeccable style—Emma Frost is ready to hit the table! 💎❄️ Painting her all-white palette was a fun challenge, balancing cool and warm tones to keep her sleek yet striking. The crystals on her base were especially fun—drybrushing them with emerald green gave them a rich, magical feel, and the soft glow effect on her legs and cape really ties it all together. As an anti-hero, she brings both hero and villain options to the game—can’t wait to see which side she ends up on!

From ruthless White Queen of the Hellfire Club to the sharp-witted headmistress of Xavier’s School, Emma’s journey is one of redemption, power, and unshakable confidence. Whether manipulating minds as a villain or protecting young mutants as a leader, she always plays by her own rules. No matter what role she takes in Marvel United, she’s sure to be a force to be reckoned with!
