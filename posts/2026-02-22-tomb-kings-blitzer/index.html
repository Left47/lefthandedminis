Tomb Kings Blitzer

Royalty doesn’t sprint… it advances. Slowly. Inevitably. 🏈💀

The Tomb Kings Blitzer brings that classic Khemri energy, ancient armor, skeletal grin, and absolutely zero concern for your offensive strategy. He’s not here to dodge. He’s here to remove you from the pitch and then pose like he’s been doing it for 3,000 years.

For this one, I wanted the gold to feel bold and sun-baked, almost ceremonial. I kept the armor warm and saturated, then grounded it with darker cloth and bone tones underneath so it didn’t turn into a single yellow blob. The desert base was the finishing touch, textured gravel, a strip of pale stone, and a dry tuft to sell that “risen from the sands” vibe.

Blood Bowl sculpts are chunky in the best way, which makes them perfect for strong highlights and readable contrast from across the table. This guy should pop under stadium lights… right before he flattens your catcher 😅
