“Laughter Echoes in the Dark”

Some nights in Gotham feel a little too quiet, like the city is holding its breath. That’s when Harley Quinn tends to show up, delighted to turn someone else’s plan into her own little circus. In the Hush storyline she’s caught in the middle again, bouncing between chaos, loyalty, and whatever punchline feels right in the moment.

For this mini I leaned into the classic red and black scheme, keeping her grin bright and the shadows sharp so she pops against the base. The sculpt’s pose begged for a punch of energy, so I pushed the highlights a little hotter than usual and played around with the toxic greens underneath for contrast. Simple palette, fun details, and a character who never needs much help stealing a scene 💥🎭
