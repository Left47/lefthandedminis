🔥 Ghost Rider — about as grimdark as I get with the Marvel United crew. I went for subtle variations of black, charcoal, and purple to contrast the bright flames and skull. The fire itself was wet blended (my usual technique) with a touch of airbrushed OSL to push the glow just a bit further. Finished up with some metallic drybrushing to make that chrome really pop!
