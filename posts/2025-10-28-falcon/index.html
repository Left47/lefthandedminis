Falcon (Sam Wilson)

🦅 Wings this big deserved their own paint session.

Always love the chance to work in darker skin tones—hopefully I did Sam justice when I worked on him. His wings were a project all their own, but I’m happy with the final mix of metallic shine and battlefield grit (he’s been fighting a war, after all).

The sculpt is so dynamic it really pops on the table—feels like he’s about to launch right off the board. Still wish Season 3 had given us Joaquin’s Falcon now that Sam’s wearing the stars and stripes as Cap. 🇺🇸✨
