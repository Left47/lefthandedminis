Werewolf by Night

The moon rises, and the hunt begins... 🌕🐺

Werewolf by Night stalks the shadows of the Marvel universe (a cursed hero fighting to keep the beast within from taking over). One of the more underrated monster-era characters, but such a fun sculpt to paint.

This was my first time using @thearmypainter Speedpaints as a base layer to move faster and I was really impressed with how they performed. Even before adding highlights and edge details, the fur and muscle definition were already looking great. I hoped to get him done before Halloween, but life had other plans 🎃
