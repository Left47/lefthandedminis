Commander Shepard (FemShep)

Commander Shepard (FemShep), steady aim, impossible odds, and the weight of the galaxy resting on one trigger pull.

For this one, I leaned into cooler blues and steel tones to sell the N7 armor, keeping the palette grounded and military rather than flashy. I’m happy with how the overall silhouette reads, especially against the darker base and subtle lava cracks. A good reminder that restraint often does more work than over-rendering, particularly on iconic characters like this.

Painted from the Mass Effect board game by @modiphius_entertainment.
