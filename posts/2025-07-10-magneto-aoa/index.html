Magneto, Age of Apocalypse

"Charles dreamed of coexistence. I lead a war for survival."
— Magneto, Age of Apocalypse

In a world where Professor X died before founding the X-Men, Magneto took up his dream—and made it his own. As Apocalypse tore across the globe, culling the weak and remaking the world in his image, Magneto stood as Earth’s last line of defense. Leader of the X-Men, protector of mutantkind, and father figure to a new generation of heroes.

This mini captures that powerful shift in his story—still armored in crimson and rage, but driven by love and loss instead of vengeance. I pushed the reds, layered cool purples into the shadows, and gave his war-torn base some gritty metal tones to echo the shattered world he’s trying to save.More angles in the carousel. AoA Magneto deserves the spotlight.
