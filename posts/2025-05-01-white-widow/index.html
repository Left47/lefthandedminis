Marvel United · May 1, 2025

White Widow

❄️💥 White Widow has entered the field—and she’s bringing the frost with her. I had a blast painting this Marvel United mini, leaning into icy tones to give her a stealthy, wintry vibe. Her suit is a mix of crisp whites and subtle blues, meant to evoke the chill of the tundra and the cold precision she brings to any mission. That snow-covered base? Inspired by Russian wilderness ops—complete with cracked terrain and exposed earth underneath for a bit of drama.

She might not always be in the spotlight, but Yelena Belova brings a relentless edge to the team. She's less about flashy heroics and more about getting the job done. Really happy with how the contrasts and subtle shading came out here. She’s now ready to dive into a mission… or maybe just dropkick a villain off a rooftop.
