Donatello

Each Turtle got a unique shade green and Donnie’s might be my favorite. A base coat of @vallejocolors "Refractive Green" under "Dirty Grey" for that perfect tech-genius vibe. 🟣💡 His custom base keeps it subtle: a single broken robot arm hinting at late-night gadgeteering.

Mini by @polymorph_miniatures, base inspired by @mugsofdoomminis, and Vallejo Diorama Water Effects for that murky sewer water.
