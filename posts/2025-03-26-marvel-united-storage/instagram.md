Come nerd out with me about how I store all this Marvel United content (last pic shows every box I have).

My priority was 1. ease of setup and transport for basic games, 2. ease of setup for expansions, and 3. decor quality for my office shelf. What do you think?? I’m in the process of magnetizing the blue boxes so I can fit all the cards and minis just in those.

Here’s my current solution:
• 3 Core Boxes are “grab and go” for each season
• Custom foam insert holds ~2-3 times more characters (minis + cards) than originally
• Each box has all the elements needed for a game setup, including tokens and mission cards
• Each box has a variety of my favorite heroes, villains, and locations from that season
• Magnetic storage section keeps the minis from colliding

Wooden case holds all expansion gameplay content
• Cards are stored vertically in deck-bags for quick access
• Each deck-bag is labeled with the number of the mini box where the character is stored
• Upper tray for Locations and Villain Dashboards
• Box cover stickers on the lid for fun

Blue Storage Boxes hold all minis
• Each has foam spaces specific to the characters stored
• Each is numbered on the outside
• Inside each lid is a key with the character locations in that box
