Apocalypse

I am as far beyond mutants as they are beyond you.

Just wrapped up this Marvel United Apocalypse, and I had a blast leaning into that classic '90s cartoon vibe. I went full Saturday morning villain with bright pink energy coils and that iconic blue-and-silver armor. The chibi sculpt made it extra fun to exaggerate the color contrast and give him some real presence on the table.

Not the most complex model, but one of the most nostalgic. Who else grew up watching X-Men: The Animated Series and hearing that booming voice echo across time and space?
