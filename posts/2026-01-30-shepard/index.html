Commander Shepard

Commander Shepard, doing what Shepard does best, standing on the edge of a hostile world with a gun in one hand and a terrible choice in the other. The N7 armor carries a lot of the story here, grounded sci-fi, practical, a little beat up, and unmistakably human in a galaxy that rarely is.

Painted from Mass Effect: The Board Game – Priority: Hagalaz by Modiphius Entertainment. The OSL is a bit meh and still something I’m dialing in, but I’m especially proud of the freehand N7 logo on his chest. Small detail, high risk, and one of those moments where slowing down actually paid off 🚀
