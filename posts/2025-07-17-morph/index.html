Morph

Hey there, multiverse weirdos. Name’s Morph. Used to be the class clown… now I’m the last laugh in a world gone to hell.

Apocalypse broke the rules, so I broke my face into whatever shape gets the job done. Sure, I still crack wise — but these days the jokes come with a body count. This version of me? Painted up nice and grim, just like home. The yellow gloves? Gotta have a little pop of color in a reality full of rubble and regret.

Big thanks to @cmongames for bringing my twisted timeline to the table. And to the painter who managed to capture my devastating good looks in miniature.
