Sinistrous Mini Chronicles: Captain Carter 🇬🇧🛡️🎨

Another step into the Multiverse! Captain Carter is finished, bringing her bold colors and unbreakable resolve to the tabletop. This sculpt from Marvel United: Multiverse really pops with dynamic energy, and I had a blast bringing her to life.
🔹 Painting Notes:
* Getting that Union Jack crisp was the trickiest part—painting lines on a curved surface always keeps things interesting!
* Used a deep navy base for her suit, layering up to a strong blue highlight for contrast.
* Added subtle shading to the shield to emphasize its iconic design.
* Her hair got a mix of warm browns with a touch of red undertones to match her animated appearance. 

Now she’s ready to take on Emperor Doom, Kang, or whatever timeline-threatening villain comes next!
