Hush – the mastermind behind it all in DC Super Heroes United. 🩸
His identity stays in the shadows while he pulls the strings, setting Gotham’s villains against the Bat. One of his coldest moves? Directing Ivy to take control of Superman — turning the Man of Steel into a wrecking ball against his own friends. 💀

This one was a blast to paint—even if he’s mostly just different shades of brown. Those long leather dusters are perfect for washes, grime, and highlights to bring the character to life.
