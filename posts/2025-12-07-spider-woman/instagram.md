Spider-Woman from Marvel United

Jessica Drew has one of those origins that feels equal parts spy thriller and superhero saga, a mix of HYDRA experiments, double agents, and a powerset that sits somewhere between venom blasts and classic wall-crawling. She always looks like she’s mid-mission, even in chibi form, which makes her a fun one to bring to life.

For this mini, I leaned into the bright reds and yellows, keeping the suit crisp while letting the hair stay wild and high-contrast. The sculpt’s motion helps everything read cleanly, so I kept the highlights tight and pushed the face and mask details just enough to pop. A simple base finished things off without pulling focus from the pose. 🕷️✨
