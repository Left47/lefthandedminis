Shadow King

Shadow King, one of the oldest and most terrifying psychic threats the X-Men have ever faced. A being of pure malice that thrives on fear and control, wearing a charming smile and a fine suit just to make the horror worse.

I leaned into that contrast with a monstrous form in a clean khaki suit and a hint of Middle Eastern flair. I’m still learning my way around the airbrush, but I wanted the blending to feel like light khaki in outdoor sunlight... warm, dusty highlights and deep shadows that hint at the desert where his power first took root.
