Moon Girl & Devil Dinosaur, WIP

🦖💙💛 Moon Girl & Devil Dinosaur have officially entered the “I’ve been working on this forever” stage of painting. 😂

The upside of a big-iture is that it’s an incredible centerpiece. The downside is that every time I think I’m almost done, I find another area that needs highlights, cleanup, or just a little more love.

Moon Girl is finally starting to come together, and Devil Dinosaur is getting closer to the bold comic book look I was aiming for. Still plenty left to do, but it’s exciting to see them looking more like a team with every session.

Sometimes the biggest projects just take patience… and a lot more paint than you expected. 🎨
