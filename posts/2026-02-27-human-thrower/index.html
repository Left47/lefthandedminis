Human Thrower

Under the stadium lights, every throw starts a story…

The Human Thrower is the quiet engine of a Blitz Bowl offense, not the flashiest player on the pitch, but the one who keeps the drive alive when the cage starts to grind forward. Accurate, reliable, and just a little bit heroic when the pass finally connects. 🏈

For this one I aimed squarely at the box art, but somewhere along the way he started giving strong New York Giants energy and honestly… I do not hate it. The new Vallejo TMM line absolutely carried the armor here, those blue metallics are ridiculously smooth and way too fun to paint with. I kept the rest fairly tight to let the armor pop, and I’m continuing to refine my process as this Blood Bowl squad comes together.
