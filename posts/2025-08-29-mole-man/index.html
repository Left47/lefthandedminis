Mole Man

Painted up Mole Man after spotting him in the Fantastic Four movie 👀. Went with some fun, punchy colors on this one—especially that bright teal cloak and glowing greens. Honestly, he looks right at home against a dark background… kind of his natural state in the depths beneath the Earth 🌑🪨.

Also gave my new macro lens a spin here with some direct lighting—really happy with how much sharper the details pop compared to my usual setup!
