The Joker

Chaos Has a Favorite Color ☣️💚 (Joker: DC United)

Some nights in Gotham feel a little too quiet… until he shows up, grinning like he knows the punchline to a joke no one else wants to hear. In Batman: Hush, Joker isn’t just another chaotic detour, he’s a pressure point. His sudden appearance after a key character is shot pushes Batman right to the edge, testing the line between justice and vengeance (and reminding you that Joker’s real superpower is knowing exactly which emotional wounds to poke).

I had fun pushing the radioactive vibe on this one, playing with slime textures and letting the airbrush handle most of the glow creeping up his suit. The green-to-purple transitions were a nice challenge, especially around the coat folds and boots, and the ooze let me experiment with some extra-sickly highlights. Still learning to balance that neon pop without going full rave… but it worked this time.
