Geralt on Roach

First time painting a rider on horseback—and who better than Geralt on Roach? 🐴⚔️ This crowdfunding exclusive mini and player board from The Witcher: Path of Destiny was a blast to work on. At 32mm, these minis are way smaller than the chunky Marvel United style I'm used to, so this one really pushed my detail work. Roach gave me a run for my money, but I think the White Wolf and his trusty steed are ready for the Path.
