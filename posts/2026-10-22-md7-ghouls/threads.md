A squirrel instead of a Viking 🐿️ My first ever Star Player was supposed to be Ivar Eriksson, until I learned I couldn't afford him (a rule I probably should have read). Greg walked me through the rules, helped me find an alternative, and even lent me his Akhorne the Squirrel.

He took six block dice, a knockdown and a trip to the pavement and kept getting back up. The rest of the Grenadiers lost 2–0 to the Ghouls, with zero completed passes in the sun. Season over at 2–5.
