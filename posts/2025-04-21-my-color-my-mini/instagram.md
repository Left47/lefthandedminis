Here's my take on @elisas.minis #mycolormymini challenge, where the rules are simple: stick to your one Pantone color and its complement (or triad). The color generator handed me Melon Dream (#FF9A8A) which is basically Salmon Pink. Naturally, I chose to paint a world-threatening AI robot. 🤖💅 I leaned all the way in and gave this murderbot a pastel Easter glow-up. He may look like a macaron, but he's still here to end humanity.
