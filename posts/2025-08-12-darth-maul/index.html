Darth Maul

When I was 16 Darth Maul was the coolest thing I’d ever seen. ❤️🖤
I even dressed up as him for Halloween, double-bladed lightsaber and all.

So my first Shatterpoint mini had to be Maul. 🔥 I painted my first ever lightsaver, tried makeup sponges for the lighting and some glazing effects on the beams. I'm pretty happy with the glow! The face still isn’t quite where I want it, but I’m calling it done and moving on to the next project.
