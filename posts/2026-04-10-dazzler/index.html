Dazzler

She turns sound into light, and somehow still makes it look effortless… ✨

Dazzler has always been one of those characters that leans all the way into style, disco energy, and pure visual spectacle. Translating that into a tiny chibi sculpt felt like a perfect excuse to play with color, contrast, and a little bit of glow magic.

I kept the base scheme bright and punchy, then experimented with some pink OSL coming off the hand effect to push that “light show” feel. It’s always a balance though, how much glow adds to the story vs how much it starts to overpower the details you worked on.

So now I’m curious… which version are you going with?
Clean and classic, or full OSL glow-up? 💖⚡
