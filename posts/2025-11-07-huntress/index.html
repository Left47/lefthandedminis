Huntress

🖤💜🖤 Huntress from the Hush box — I don’t know her whole backstory, but I do know she looks ready to drop in from the rooftops with her crossbow. Had fun pushing some lighter tones up from the deep blacks and purples, and the sculpt even snuck in some nice touches like those rounded clay shingles and exposed brick. Details like that make the whole mini pop.

Paints for this one were mostly @thearmypainter with some @vallejocolors highlights.
