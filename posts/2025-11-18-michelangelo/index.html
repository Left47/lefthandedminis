Michelangelo

Mikey’s my favorite turtle, but this mini didn’t feel like it fully did that justice at first — so I had to fix that 🛹💥

I scratch-built a skateboard for him and rigged it so it looks like he’s flipping or catching it mid-fight. The before-and-after really shows how much that little prop brought the mini to life.

Anyone have good tricks for “invisible” supports when you want to make something float?
