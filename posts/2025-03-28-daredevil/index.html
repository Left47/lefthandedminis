Daredevil

With Daredevil: Born Again delivering some incredible moments, I had to get my Marvel United Daredevil mini painted! Went for his classic red look but leaned into darker accents to match the card art—felt fitting for a hero who thrives in the shadows.

Loving the show so far, and painting this felt like the perfect way to channel that excitement. Do you paint your minis to match the box art, or do you go for something more creative? Let me know! 👇
