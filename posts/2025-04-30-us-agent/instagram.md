U.S. Agent reporting for duty. 

Wrapped up this mini from the Marvel United: Multiverse stretch goals box just as the hype starts building for Thunderbolts. Gotta be ready!

I’m especially happy with the highlights I put on the dark suit - I'm not one for long blending sessions, so 2-3 shades is my norm. Comes out with a bit of a cell-shaded look, which I don't mind at all especially on these minis!

That said, round shields continue to be my painting nemesis. Getting those clean lines right always tests me. But hey, if Walker can stubbornly throw himself into each fight, I can stubbornly keep painting these shields until I win the war.
