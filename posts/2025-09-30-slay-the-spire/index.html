Slay the Spire minis

Throwback climb up the Spire 🖌️✨

Painting these Slay the Spire board-game minis was a blast, but The Defect stole the show, both on the table and at the hobby desk. I experimented with glass-effect orbs for the first time and loved how the blues and highlights turned out.

⚡💎Big thanks to @contentiongames for bringing this to the tabletop and to @megacrit for creating such an addictive game in the first place!

🎨 Paints: @vallejocolors Game Color set for those rich reds & icy blues
