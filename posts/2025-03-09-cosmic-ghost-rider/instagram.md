🔥💀 Cosmic Ghost Rider 💀🔥 from Marvel United: Multiverse is all about over-the-top energy—flames, skulls, and sci-fi firepower cranked to 11. The sculpt is packed with detail, from the blazing chains to the massive space guns, making it a dream for painting fiery effects and metallics.

I leaned into the contrast between his ethereal flames and the deep, dark blues of his armor. The skull got a glowing, otherworldly look, and the weapons? Straight out of a heavy metal album cover. This was one of those minis where the more dramatic, the better.
