Galactus

📸 Finally got the Big Boy of Season 3 in front of the camera. Here's Galactus in all his planet-devouring glory! Had to rig up a larger light box just to fit him in the frame 😅. He's been sitting behind me on the shelf for a while now, grimly attending zoom meetings and getting compliments from the folks who appreciate his presence!

I'm really happy with how he turned out. The @turbodork paints really give him a nice shine and seeing him next to his heralds really showcases the cosmic size of this "mini". So much fun having him come together and I can't wait to see him on the table (and in Fantastic Four: First Steps in a few months)! 🌌🟣🪐
