Spider-Man (Hobie Brown)

Spider-Man (yes, Hobie prefers that over Spider-Punk 😎) is ready to bring the noise! 🧷⚡ This alt universe rocker was hard but fun to paint. He's packed with tiny details and fiddly bits that made it a real challenge. I really enjoyed layering the blues on his jacket for that lived-in denim look and added some gloss varnish to his guitar for that perfect punk-rock shine. One of the most dynamic Marvel United sculpts I've painted so far! 🎸💥
