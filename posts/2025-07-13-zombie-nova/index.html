Zombie Nova

🧟‍♂️ Check out my latest undead hero from the Zombicide line!

I picked Zombie Nova for a painting class a while back, mainly for the awesome base: fire, smoke, and the wreckage of a crashing spaceship? Hard to resist.

For the zombie flesh, I tried a pale purple tone to give it a raw, decayed look… but I’m still experimenting. Might shift to a sickly green in future models to get that classic infected vibe. 🤔

I'm definitely planning to tackle more of the Marvel zombies soon so if you've got any favorite zombie flesh recipes or tips drop them below! 👇
