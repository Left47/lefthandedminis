Cable

🔫🎨 Here's Cable from the Marvel United X-Force expansion! Beyond his massive guns and glowing eye, what really makes Nathan Summers fascinating is his wild family tree.

Born as the son of Cyclops (Scott Summers) and a clone of Jean Grey (Madelyne Pryor), Cable was sent to the future to survive a techno-organic virus. He grew up in a post-apocalyptic world, became a battle-hardened warrior, and returned to the present as the ultimate soldier to prevent the very future he was raised in. Talk about complicated family dynamics! Oh, and let’s not forget he’s also the half-brother of Rachel Summers and the genetic template for Stryfe, his evil clone. The Summers family never does anything halfway!

Really happy with how this one turned out—especially capturing that gritty, time-worn look.
