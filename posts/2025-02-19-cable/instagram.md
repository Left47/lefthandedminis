He grew up in a post-apocalyptic world, became a battle-hardened warrior, and returned to the present as the ultimate soldier to prevent the very future he was raised in. Talk about complicated family dynamics! Oh, and let’s not forget he’s also the half-brother of Rachel Summers and the genetic template for Stryfe, his evil clone. The Summers family never does anything halfway!
