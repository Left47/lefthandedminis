Shuri (Black Panther)

Closed out the Multiverse core box with Shuri in the Black Panther suit. I wasn’t super inspired by the mini at first, but I ended up enjoying the challenge of building up those “black” surfaces with careful layering of grays.

True black can feel flat on the table, so I used warm-to-neutral grays with a touch of highlight to keep the suit looking dynamic and still shadowy. Her stealthy silhouette pops just enough without losing that panther vibe.

More pics in the carousel to show off how those layers read from different angles.
