Leonardo

🐢 Here's Leonardo from the fan-designed TMNT United game is ready to slice and dice—this time on a custom sewer base I built from scratch. 💪 These are the turtles I know from my childhood. It may not match the homebrew card art but it's how they look in my head!

Got out of my comfort zone to sculpt the tunnel floor from cork and @thearmypainter Green Stuff, use @vallejocolors Diorama FX Water Effects for that grimy underground flow, and even added broken ooze canisters for a proper Turtles vibe. 💙⚔️ Big thanks to @polymorph_miniatures for the printed minis that kicked off the whole project.

Painted as always with @vallejocolors & @thearmypainter, sealed for maximum turtle power.
