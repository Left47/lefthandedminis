Marvel United has a lot of content. Core boxes, expansions, promo packs, hundreds of minis and even more cards. For a while my collection was a stack of retail boxes, and setting up a game meant opening five of them to find one villain. This is the system I landed on.

![Office shelf with three Marvel United core boxes between two stacks of numbered navy storage boxes, and a paint rack on the shelf below](img:marvel-united-shelf.jpg "The office shelf: three grab-and-go core boxes between the numbered mini boxes.")

## What I was optimizing for

In order:

1. **Easy setup and transport for a basic game.**
2. **Easy setup when I want expansion content.**
3. **Looking good on my office shelf.**

That order matters. If a system looks great but makes setup slow, the games don't get played.

## The short version

Three core boxes are "grab and go" kits, one per season, each with everything needed for a full game. A wooden case holds every expansion card, filed so you can find anything fast. Numbered blue boxes hold the rest of the minis, with a key inside each lid that says who's where.

## Part 1: grab-and-go core boxes

Each season's core box is set up so I can grab one and play.

- **A custom foam insert** holds about two to three times more characters (minis plus cards) than the original insert.
- **Everything for a full game** lives in the box, including tokens and mission cards.
- **A curated roster:** a variety of my favorite heroes, villains and locations from that season, not just the ones that came in the box.
- **A magnetic storage section** keeps the minis from colliding in transit.

![Two open core boxes packed with minis and cards in custom inserts](post:2025-03-26-marvel-united-storage/02.jpg "Core boxes: one per season, ready to go.")

## Part 2: the wooden case for expansion cards

All expansion gameplay content lives in one wooden case.

- **Cards stand vertically in deck bags** for quick access.
- **Each deck bag is labeled** with the number of the mini box where that character lives.
- **An upper tray** holds locations and villain dashboards.
- **Box cover stickers on the lid**, because it should be fun too.

![Wooden case with rows of labeled card bags and box-art stickers on the lid](post:2025-03-26-marvel-united-storage/03.jpg "One case for every expansion card, cross-referenced to the mini boxes.")

## Part 3: numbered blue boxes for minis

- **Foam spaces sized to the characters stored**
- **A number on the outside of each box**
- **A key inside each lid** with the character locations in that box

The trick that ties it together is the cross-reference: a card bag says "box 4," box 4's lid says where the mini is. No digging.

![Foam insert with cutouts holding painted and unpainted minis](img:foam-storage.jpg "Foam cutouts sized to each mini.")

## Where it went next: magnets

When I first shared this, I was in the middle of magnetizing the minis so I could fit all the cards and minis into fewer boxes. That turned into [magnetizing about 200 minis in about 20 minutes](../magnetize-200-minis/), with the minis standing on magnetic sheet in stackable boxes instead of foam.

**2026 update:** this is basically still my system. Two things changed: the blue boxes are magnetized now instead of foam, and I moved the cards out of the wooden case into card boxes that sit inline with everything else.

![A box of magnetized minis standing in rows](img:magnetized-box.jpg "The magnetized version: minis standing on magnetic sheet.")

## Borrow the ideas, not the exact setup

You don't need this exact gear for any game with a lot of content:

- **Build "grab and go" kits** for the way you usually play.
- **Separate what you always need from what you sometimes need.**
- **Number everything and write a key.** Future you will not remember which box has Moon Knight.
- **Cross-reference cards to minis** if the game pairs them.

## Checklist

- Decide your priorities (setup, expansions, looks)
- One complete kit per "normal" game
- Cards filed vertically and labeled
- Boxes numbered with a key in the lid
- Magnets or foam so minis don't collide
