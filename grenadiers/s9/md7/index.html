Win or go home. I meant to debut my first ever Star Player, Ivar Eriksson, but the budget said no, so a squirrel and a keg came instead. Akhorne refused to stay down, the sun switched off the passing game, and two fast Ghouls ended the season.

On air Cabalvision match report

With Jim Johnson and Bob Bifford

JIMGood evening, Blood Bowl fans, and it's win-or-go-home under the floodlights! The Holy Hand Grenadiers need two points tonight to keep their playoff hopes breathing, and standing in the way... the shambling, howling, regenerating Ghouls Just Wanna Have Fun.

BOBAnd Jim, the Grenadiers were supposed to walk out with Ivar Eriksson tonight. Instead they've walked out with... a squirrel. And a keg!

JIMTurns out the league's accountants only let a team crack open fifty thousand from the treasury for a Star, Bob. Akhorne the Squirrel it is, and to be fair, a keg of Blitzer's Best does wonders for the knocked-out lads on the bench.

BOBIt's a dirty one early! Boots the Zombie gets his boot in on 32 minutes. That's the Dirty Player, and the Ghouls are already putting the slipper into downed Grenadiers.

JIMAnd they can't get rid of the squirrel, Bob! Two hits on Akhorne already, six block dice and a knockdown, and he's still standing. He hits the pavement and hops straight back onto his stump. There's talk in the stands of Gnome blood.

BOBLong, grinding first stretch, and then on 139 minutes Punkin' Head, the Ghoul Runner, slips through and scores! One–nil Ghouls!

JIMAnd would you look at that sky, Bob. The sun's come out hard. Very Sunny! Minus one on every pass for the rest of the day, and the Grenadiers are a PASSING team!

BOBSecond half, and Akhorne's flattened AGAIN... double one on the armour! That squirrel is made of oak, Jim.

JIMMore boots from the Zombies: Prancer on 161, Blue Buscetta on 174. Then the Ghouls start finding the squires. Lips crunches Siegbert Schott on 212, Badly Hurt. Grabby the Wraith does the same to Ralf Goldwasser on 229.

BOBBut the Grail Knight answers! Alejandra, the Change Warden, levels a Ghoul for a casualty on 240! Mind you, half these fellows just get back up again. Regeneration, Jim, it's very unsporting.

JIMAnd there's the dagger. Fido the Werewolf, 245 minutes, touchdown! Two–nil Ghouls, and the Grenadiers' season is slipping away.

BOBPrancer gets one more casualty in before the whistle for good measure, but the Grenadier Apothecary patches that one up. Not a single completed pass for the knights tonight, Jim. Between the sunshine and those Werewolves, the air game never got off the ground.

JIMMVP honours to Gracien De Cobie for the Grenadiers, and to Lips for the Ghouls. Final score: Ghouls two, Grenadiers nil, and that's the end of the road for the Holy Hand Grenadiers in Season 9.

BOBThey came for a Viking, Jim. They left with a squirrel. Some nights, Nuffle just laughs.
