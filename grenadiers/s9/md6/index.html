The rematch. Two Treemen, a Master Chef pinching rerolls, and a game decided by dodges. One Halfling touchdown was all it took.

On air Cabalvision match report

With Jim Johnson and Bob Bifford

JIMGood evening, Blood Bowl fans! It's a rematch: the Grenadiers beat these Halflings two–nil back on Matchday 1, and the Lucharitos have brought reinforcements. A keg of Blitzer's Best and a Master Chef rattling pots on the sideline.

BOBThe Chef, Jim! Every half that little cook is pinching re-rolls off the Grenadier bench with the smell of pie. You can't hit what you can't re-roll!

JIMKnights start sharp, though. Rogerin D'Armignac finds his man downfield on 26 minutes. That's the passing game doing its job.

BOBAnd then the tree walks in. Locustthorn the Treeman plants himself in front of Rogerin and... oh, that's a thick branch to the helmet. Thrower stretchered off!

JIMNine minutes later the tree does it again. Squire Kunigunde Steinberger flattened, Badly Hurt. Two swings, two casualties. That's the whole Halfling offence right there, Bob: one enormous plant.

BOBThe Grenadiers try to bully the ball through the Halfling line, and the Treeman stuns the carrier! Ball loose, Halflings swarming it like it's the last pie at the buffet.

JIMA knight scoops it up on a six, Bob, a SIX... and then trips over his own spurs on the dodge. Ball back on the turf.

BOBLittle Lambert Tinyfoot tries to get his own boot in at 74. Nothing doing, Jim, squire armour holds.

JIMSecond half, and here's a squire with ideas! She dodges out, goes for it, goes for it AGAIN, and Wrestles the Halfling ball carrier into the dirt! Both of them flat on their backs!

BOBMoments later a Grail Knight tries the same dodge, fails it, and knocks himself clean out. Burned a re-roll on the way down, too. Nuffle giveth, Jim.

JIMAnd credit to the Grenadiers, they hold the Halflings out for over two hours of match clock... until the 140th minute. Clothair Mugwort, the Halfling Catcher, scampers through and dots it down. One–nil Lucharitos!

BOBGrenadiers throw the kitchen sink. Gracien De Cobie connects one more pass at 149, but the clock beats them. That's the ballgame.

JIMMVP honours to Amynta for the Grenadiers, and to (who else) Locustthorn for the home side. Final: Lucharitos one, Grenadiers nil.

BOBLost to a tree and a pie, Jim. Long bus ride home.
